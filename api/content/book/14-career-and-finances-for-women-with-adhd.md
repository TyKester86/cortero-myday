# Chapter 14: Career and Finances for Women with ADHD

The career and financial experience of women with ADHD is shaped by a painful paradox: intelligence and capability that produce high expectations, combined with executive function deficits that undermine performance relative to those expectations. The result is a chronic performance gap — not between what is done and what others can do, but between what is done and what the individual knows she is capable of. This gap generates shame that compounds over time and can become the primary obstacle to career advancement.

## Workplace Accommodations

Under the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) in the United States, individuals with ADHD may be entitled to reasonable workplace accommodations. These can include extended time on written work, flexible work scheduling, remote work options, written rather than verbal instructions, noise-reducing accommodations, and the provision of assistive technology. Requesting accommodations requires disclosure to HR or a disability office and documentation of diagnosis, but does not require sharing diagnosis information with supervisors or colleagues.

## Financial Patterns and Systems

Women with ADHD show characteristic financial patterns: forgotten bills producing late fees and credit score damage, emotional spending as a dopamine-seeking or emotional regulation strategy, under-charging for services and under-negotiating salary due to the conflict-avoidance driven by RSD, and financial disorganization that creates anxiety without clarity. The financial automation stack — every recurring expense on autopay, savings transferred automatically on payday, investment contributions automated — removes the human decision layer from financial management and allows the system to function regardless of the executive function available on any given day.

## KEY INSIGHT

You are not underperforming because you are less capable. You are underperforming in an environment designed for a different nervous system. Your intelligence is real. Your capability is real. The gap between them and your output is not a character flaw — it is a systems problem, and systems problems have systems solutions.
