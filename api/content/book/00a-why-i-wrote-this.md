# Why I Wrote This

I did not write this book from a clinic, a laboratory, or a lecture hall. I wrote it from a kitchen table — from a life where ADHD is not a chapter heading but the daily weather.

Everything in these pages was built the hard way: tried in a real house, with real mornings that fell apart, real evenings of good intentions and undone tasks, real shame after the third planner in a row stopped working by February. The systems in this book are the ones that survived contact with real life. The ones that did not survive are not in here.

I am not a guru and this is not a memoir of triumph. I am someone who got tired of being told the problem was effort, and decided to find out what actually works for a brain like this one — then wrote down everything that did, and threw away everything that did not.

If you have spent years believing you were lazy, or broken, or simply not trying hard enough, I wrote this for you. You were never the problem. You just never had the right operating system.

This book is that operating system. And it works best when it lives in your pocket, not just on your shelf — which is why I built the MyDay app to run this entire system for you, day by day. You will find it at the end of this book. But first, the foundation: understanding the machine you are working with.
